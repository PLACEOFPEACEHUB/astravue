Child Safety Standards

Astravue — last updated: November 11, 2026

Astravue is a creator platform for watching, sharing, and discovering videos, photos, and posts. These standards set out how Astravue prohibits, responds to, and reports child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform. They apply to all content and behaviour on Astravue, on our apps and on astravue.net.

1. Our commitment

Astravue has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — any visual depiction of a minor engaging in sexually explicit conduct, including photos, videos, and computer-generated imagery — is strictly prohibited on Astravue. We prohibit anyone from creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, or distributing CSAM or any content or behaviour that exploits or endangers children, and we act on reports of it as described below.

2. Prohibited content and behaviour

No one may use Astravue to:

  • Create, upload, or distribute child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind.
  • Solicit, request, or exchange sexual content involving a minor, or attempt to do so.
  • Groom a child — for example, befriending or building trust with a child online to facilitate sexual contact or the exchange of sexual imagery, online or offline.
  • Sexualise a minor, including imagery or commentary that depicts, encourages, or promotes the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.
  • Sextort a child — threatening or blackmailing a child using real or alleged access to their intimate images.
  • Advertise, solicit, or facilitate the trafficking of a child for sexual exploitation.
  • Engage in any other predatory behaviour directed at a child.

3. Who may use Astravue

You must be at least 13 years old to use Astravue. If you are under 18, you must have the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Astravue is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us at support@astravue.net and we will delete it.

4. How to report a child safety concern

  • In the app: open the menu on any post or video and tap Report, then choose Child safety. Your report goes directly to our moderation team. You can also report from the Support section of the app.
  • By email: write to support@astravue.net with a link to the content or the name of the account concerned.
  • In an emergency: if a child is in immediate danger, contact your local law enforcement first. In the United States, suspected online child exploitation can also be reported to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.

You do not need an Astravue account to email us a child safety concern.

5. How we respond

Every report is reviewed by a member of the Astravue team. Reports marked as child safety concerns are given priority over other reports. When we become aware of content that violates these standards, we remove it. When we confirm child sexual abuse material, we remove the content, preserve the information needed for reporting, and report it to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional authority, as required by applicable law. We also cooperate with law enforcement requests as required by law. Accounts that violate these standards are subject to action under our Terms of Use, up to and including termination of the account.

Astravue is committed to protecting young people and responding to reports of harm.
